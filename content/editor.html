COMMAND GUIDE

Speak your next position.

Describe your placements naturally. Say “let’s do black next” to set the active color, or name a color for a single piece. If a placement is unclear, the editor asks you to clarify.

Place a piece
“Put a white king on e5”
Choose a color
Say “switch to black” or “let’s do white next”, or click a color. The active color carries over to the next recording.
Place several pieces
“For black, a king on a5 and pawns on d2, d3 and d5”
Use phonetic coordinates
“White rook on alpha one”
Remove a piece
“Clear e4” or “Take the queen off e4”
Move a piece
“Move e2 to e4” or “e2, e4”
Read several moves
“e2, e4, e7, e5, g1, f3”
Board commands
“Clear board”, “Starting position”, “Flip board”, “Undo”, “Redo”

Voice input records up to 60 seconds and sends audio to Groq for transcription, then sends the transcript to a Groq-hosted language model to interpret placements. Pieces appear after you finish recording. Chess shorthand such as Qd8 is accepted. Use explicit piece names and squares. Click Start recording and Stop recording, or hold Space and release to finish. Shortcuts are inactive while typing. Requires microphone permission and a configured model connection. Typed commands also work.
